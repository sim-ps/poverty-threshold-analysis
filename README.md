# Philippine Per-Capita Poverty Thresholds, 2018–2023

[View the interactive Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/simran.sujanani/viz/PovertyThresholdAnalysis_17926140137830/FocusedComparisonRegionsIIIandXII?publish=yes)

## Project overview

This project explores how the annual per-capita poverty threshold changed across the Philippines in 2018, 2021, and 2023. I created five connected Tableau analyses to compare national and regional patterns, including a focused comparison of Regions III and XII.

The analysis looks at:

- How the national threshold changed over time
- How thresholds differed across regions in 2023
- How much each region’s threshold changed from 2018 to 2023
- How the 2023 thresholds were distributed across regions
- How Regions III and XII compared

## Key findings

- The national per-capita poverty threshold increased from **PhP 25,814 in 2018** to **PhP 33,296 in 2023**.
- In 2023, **Region III had the highest threshold** at PhP 38,510, while **Region XII had the lowest** at PhP 29,379.
- All 18 regions had higher thresholds in 2023 than in 2018.

## Method and limitations

Regional change is calculated as:

`2023 threshold − 2018 threshold`

The charts describe poverty thresholds in pesos. They do not measure poverty incidence or explain why thresholds changed. Missing values were treated as missing.
