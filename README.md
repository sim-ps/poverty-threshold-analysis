# Philippine Per-Capita Poverty Thresholds, 2018–2023

[View the interactive Tableau workbook](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/simran.sujanani/viz/PovertyThresholdAnalysis_17926140137830/FocusedComparisonRegionsIIIandXII?publish=yes)

## Project
I built five connected Tableau analyses of annual per-capita poverty thresholds in the Philippines: the national trend, 2023 regional comparison, regional change from 2018 to 2023, regional distribution, and a comparison of Regions III and XII.

## Key findings
- The national threshold rose from PhP 25,814 in 2018 to PhP 33,296 in 2023.
- In 2023, Region III had the highest regional threshold (PhP 38,510) and Region XII the lowest (PhP 29,379).
- All 18 regions had higher thresholds in 2023 than in 2018.

## Method and limitations
Regional change is calculated as the 2023 threshold minus the 2018 threshold. The charts describe poverty thresholds; they do not measure poverty incidence or explain why thresholds changed. Missing values were treated as missing.
